# The Expanse

James S. A. Corey. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ — read but not individually reviewed (see `diary/series_overview.md`).

Not great literature but so entertaining. Like watching a movie. I became caught up with the world and the characters. I enjoyed the "realistic" portrayal of a future where humanities problems haven't been solved but rather expanded in scope. The optimistic end turned these novels from a bleak prediction to one of ultimate hope for humanity.
