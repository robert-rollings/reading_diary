Another immensely entertaining entry in the [[Dungeon Crawler Carl (series)|Dungeon Crawler Carl]] series. Binnimann is starting to expand on his theme as we start to see more of the nature of society outside of Earth. It becomes clear that technological sophistication and respect for life do not go together. The universe starts to seem more and more like Ancient Rome. The Dungeon a great coliseum and the way to keep the plebs happy.

The increased length gave time for the plot to develop, the characters to come together and for deeper themes to be established. Dinnimann is growing into his style and his craft. That said Dungeon Crawler is no masterpiece. It is however immensely entertaining.
