# System Collapse by Martha Wells


Another enjoyable story from Martha Wells. Murder Bot continues to get more human with his "Neural Tissue" taking a stronger and more dominante role in his psychology. He cares, he suffers trauma and is struggling with his traumatic experiences. He is building relationships and making friends.

I'm starting to think that Murder Bot is modelled on an Autistic person. Unable to interact with other humans in a natural way but just as in need of love and able to give it.

The satire of capitalism and the drawing of the result of its natural, unregulated, progression is as witty and dark as ever. And reminds me of the worlds of [[The Expanse]]
