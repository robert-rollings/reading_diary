Enjoyable nautical romp. Hobb keeps the tension up and the character development coming. This was very much a middle novel. The scene has been set for all the threads to weave together. Satrap, Bingtown and Rain River Traders, The Veserit's and the Pirates. With a grumpy dragon thrown in. 

Hobb's themes of free will, family and loyalty all come into this. Are we loyal to our families out of love, obligation or both. Is a Live Ship a family member or a slave treated well? If all something is memory (a live ship) is it truly alive at all? These are big questions that Hobb grapples with throughout the Liveship Traders. 

Taken together with the _Farseer Trillogy_ I can see an underlying theme emerging - loyalty. Who deserves it and why and when is Loyalty earned, given and an obligation. We see it in _The Liveship Traders_ between a ship and its family, between Etta and Kennet where loyalty is one way. Freely given from love and grattitude from Etta to Kennet and manipulated further from Kennet, Etta, Wintrow and Vivacia. In the _[[Farseer Trilogy]]_ all relationships centre on Fitz as that is the only perspective we have. Fitz loyalty is split between Burich his adoptive Father, his king and his Night Eyes. He is torn between them all but ultimately it is love that guides his way.

For Hobb to keep to her theme a Crisis must be coming for Vivacia. She must understand what love is and who is deserving of it before she gifts her loyalty.
