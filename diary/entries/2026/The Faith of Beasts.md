This is epic scifi at its best. Wiered, huge in scale with an intrecate world, characters that are lovable and not just pieces on a game board. Corey manages to tie intamacy and scale together. Throughout he takes us to the scale of interstella travel and the next brings us down to the scale of simple interaction, a raised eyebrow, a kiss given, a glance or not. 

Corey uses character as a means to tell his story and explore ideas rather than neccessary mechanics in a plot.
